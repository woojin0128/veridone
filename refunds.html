Refunds

Last updated 2026-10-10

  • First purchase: a full refund within 14 days, no questions asked.
  • Renewals: a full refund within 7 days of the renewal charge if you forgot to cancel.
  • Lifetime licenses: a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

To ask for a refund, reply to your receipt email or contact us through the customer portal. Refunds are issued by Polar to your original payment method. When we issue a refund we also revoke the license key: Veridone’s paid features stop at its next check, and the free features keep working.

This policy does not limit any rights you have under the consumer law of your country.
