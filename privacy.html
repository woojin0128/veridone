Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-10

The plugin

Veridone runs on your computer. Its hooks, CLI and MCP server make no network requests and send no telemetry. It keeps a per-session event log (the first 500 characters of each prompt, commands run, files changed, check results and findings) in Claude Code’s plugin data folder and deletes it after 14 days. Secret values are never logged.

License checks

When you activate a store license key, and every few days afterwards, Veridone sends the key, the store’s organization ID, a random label for your computer, the Veridone version and your operating system name to Polar’s license API. Nothing about your code, prompts or files is sent. Offline keys and the free trial are checked entirely on your computer.

Purchases

Polar Software, Inc. processes orders as merchant of record and receives your payment and billing details under its privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, country and order details from Polar, and use them only to provide your license, support and invoices.

This website

This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. GitHub may log visits as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Reply to your receipt email, or open a GitHub issue asking us to contact you (do not post personal data in public).
