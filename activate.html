Activate your license

For Veridone 1.0.1 and later.

On your computer

  1. Copy your key. It is shown on the order confirmation page and in your customer portal, and looks like VERIDONE-PRO-XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXXXXXXXXX.
  2. Update the plugin so it knows the store: claude plugin update veridone@veridone.
  3. In Claude Code, run /veridone:license activate <your key>.
  4. Check it with /veridone:license status.

A key works on up to 3 computers. Veridone re-checks it in the background every few days; without a connection it keeps working for 14 days.

In CI

Add the key as a secret named VERIDONE_LICENSE_KEY. CI runs validate the key without using one of your computer slots.

- uses: woojin0128/veridone@v1
  id: veridone
  with:
    sarif: veridone.sarif
    license-key: ${{ secrets.VERIDONE_LICENSE_KEY }}
- uses: github/codeql-action/upload-sarif@v3
  if: always() && steps.veridone.outputs.sarif-file != ''
  with:
    sarif_file: veridone.sarif

Team seats

Whoever bought the Team plan assigns seats by email in the customer portal. Each person claims the seat from the invitation email and receives their own VERIDONE-TEAM-… key.

Moving to a new computer

Run /veridone:license deactivate on the old one, or remove it in the customer portal, then activate on the new one.

If something goes wrong

  • “not connected to a store yet”: update the plugin and try again.
  • “maximum number of computers”: free a slot as described above.
  • “Couldn't reach the license server”: check your connection. Behind a proxy, set NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1 (Node.js 22.21 or 24 and later).
  • Anything else: open an issue without posting your key, or reply to your receipt email.

Not ready to buy?
